## Tough Times

Occasionally, there will be really hard times at the club. For example, at the time of writing this, just a couple months ago we were in the spot where Nemo was not waterproof, we had no working sub/rack assembled because of waiting for mech to waterproof, the competition had just moved to Washington DC (and there was internal resistance even among management to even going to competition) (which required hela more planning). Just a month before comp of 2022, we didn't even have the thrusters spinning. We spent a week working on it, just to find out that two switched wires were causing the signal to be reversed or something like that.

The point is, sometimes there will be really tough times. Sometimes it will look so bleak, and you might want to quit. You start thinking: What's the point? If I just get out everything will be so much lighter.

In these times, the only response is that you just have to dial it in. I already touched on this in other pieces of documentation, but a good method for dealing with this pressure is the following:
- Exercise
- Meditation
- Journaling
- Reading

And get a hela amount of sleep. This way, you can have your body and mind recover and be strong enough to be able to deal with the pressure. Just try your best that you can; usually, there is always a way through. Even if there isn't a way through, at least you tried your best, and you learned so many invaluable lessons along the way. Either way, it's a win-win. I hope this can help you out in the future of this club.

Also, document everything you do! At the time of writing Botz has stalled for the last 7 years or so with no big progress. We've just been moving sideways instead of forwards. For this, document everything you do so that the next generation can learn the current things fast and advance our capabilities instead of spending all their time re-learning and re-making old things and moving laterally.

## Writing Good Emails

To write good emails, for example looking for new sponsors, or to communicate with your current sponsors, here's the strategy I used:
- Be extremely clear in what you want.
- Don't be afraid to ask for a lot, since you're only a high school team and you need da money.
- Use your own voice; don't try to copy someone else's email style, but instead be genuine and authentic with your own style of writing. It'll come off more clean and genuine which feels better to read.
- Genuinely be thankful for the sponsor that you're contacting. It will come through on the email. Remember that they are not obligated to give anything, and anything they give is through their own philanthropy and kindness.
- Emphasize how the partnership helps them (advertising their product at comp, providing feedback, putting their logo on website/shirt/sub, social media shoutouts, etc.)


### Tips from BumbleBee (National University of Singapore)

1. Purpose of email. This includes the title of your email and also the first sentence that tops your email. Be wise when choosing the right title to attract attention rather than being deleted off at first sight.

2. Self-introduction. Let the company knows who you are. You should describe why your team is appealing.

3. What you want. Describe what (product/services) you want from the company and how it can help your team. Tell them why you choose them rather than other companies.

4. How you can help them. This is entirely up to you. Most of the time, we publicise for the companies. In some cases, companies may want you to make a product (or services) for them that is associated to your AUV. If this happens, several email exchanges may ensue to negotiate (see next point). 

5. Offer to meet up. Get your presentation slides ready at all time. Know what you want to say when you meet up with them. 

### BumbleBee Example

Dear Mr Tan,

I am Ananya, representing Team Bumblebee from the National University of Singapore. Bumblebee is a student-led competition team that develops autonomous marine robotics for the RoboSub and RobotX maritime robotics competitions. Our team has two Autonomous Underwater Vehicles (AUV) and one Autonomous Surface Vehicle (ASV) that we are currently developing for the competitions held in July and November respectively. As our vehicles are modular and require connections between its enclosures and sensors, we require waterproof connectors for secure electrical connections.

With the help of MacArtney's sponsorship in the past we were able to achieve impressive results such as 3rd place in Robosub in 2017 and 2nd place in Robosub in 2018. Our vehicle currently uses a variety of MacArtney's products from the Subconn MicroCircular Series, Ethernet Series and Low Profile Series. Considering the upcoming competitions and our continual use of these connectors we would like to ask for your support again through a sponsorship from MacArtney in the procurement of connectors for our vehicles. As thanks, our team displays the logos of our sponsors on the vehicles themselves, as well as on our website, apparel, and journal papers. You may have a look at the details of possible sponsorship packages attached to this email. As RoboSub and RobotX are international competitions attended by teams and company representatives from many countries, this is a prime opportunity for the team to gain recognition for our sponsors.

If you are interested, we would like to arrange a meeting with you, either in person or online to share more about who we are and how we can help each other through this sponsorship. Do let me know when you might be available for such a meeting, and I will coordinate my team accordingly.

Thank you for taking the time to read this and we look forward to your favourable reply.

Best regards,
Ananya Agarwal

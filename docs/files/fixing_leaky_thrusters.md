## Fixing Leaky Thrusters

Sometimes, our VideoRay M5 motors will leak red fluid. To fix this, we unscrewed a screw at the bottom, and refilled it with Toyota Automatic Transmission Fluid (ATF) that is compatible with electric vehicles.

We also lined the edges of the thruster with cyanoacrylate (CA), which is a really strong type of glue, and we then epoxied (epoxy is another strong type of glue sort of) the edges as well. This worked temporarily, but remember that these are just temporary fixes. When it gets as bad as leaking red oil, it's better to purchase new thrusters if you are able to.

### Health

Health is the #1 priority, fr. You will not believe how easy it is to do work if you're physically and mentally healthy. Health is the most important thing. You could get into a *nice* college, but then have kidney failure and be conked out for 3 months, or you could go insane on a lack of sleep and end up putting urself in a rut. Or, u could get a nice 8-9 hours of sleep a night to *nourish* ur mind, which puts ur life in creative (easy) mode. Good mood, lots of energy, can think clearly.

How do you get good health? This is what I found helpful:
* Sleep - 8-9 hours of night. Good sleep makes u feel good. Good vibes, happiness. No sleep makes u feel bad, sad. (bars). On a serious note, sleep improves probably mostly everything in your body and mind. It'll improve your mood, make you more energetic, which makes you more productive and smart. Nice.
* Exercise - Some type of exercise so u don't turn into a slug. I'd recommend making it easy and fun, so u don't rely on discipline. (eg. do 5 squats a day, super breezy, and just do what ur comfortable with, over time it'll compound and you can scale up ur exercise accordingly if u choose to, but no pressure on urself, keep it easy and fun so it's sustainable)
* Journaling - some short notes every day, it's just interesting to see the progression of who u become over many years. Ppl change like glaciers, slowly but over time there's a huge difference/movement.
* Reading - books have a lot of wisdom. Fiction books have a lot of wisdom for who we are as people. Nonfiction books have a lot of good knowledge to know that might help. (I recommend the Three Body Problem, Atomic Habits, 80/20 Rule, Elon Musk biography, The Myth of Normal)
* Meditating - this actually helped me a lot. Helps u be more present in the moment, which makes u less stressed and more productive. Win-win. I recommend the Medito app 

Trust me, u do not wanna know what bad health feels like. Having experienced it myself, I do not wish it upon my worst enemy. Just trust me on this for real tho. 

If ur going thru something, someone's probably gone thru worse. And u might be thinking that's cap, but trust me. I thought like you once, then I experienced life, and then I understood. If u have trauma, just gotta heal from that trauma, understand that ur mind is ur friend, it's helping u (u just gotta understand what it's doing) (see the Myth of Normal). Healing from trauma is like healing from a cut - even though it might always be with you like a scar, eventually it can become something that you can heal enough from to function normally with (like a cut heals to become a scar), that even if it's still there (like a scar) it won't hurt as much (like a scar) b/c u healed from it

Do things that make u happy, do things that ur interested in, do things that u think are meaningful.

Have fun, but make sure u finish ur work first. 

### Time Management

Time management tips that helped me:
* Do the biggest tasks first, then the smaller tasks. Like filling up a jar with rocks, u put the biggest rocks first, then the smaller rocks, b/c it's easier to fit that way. (But sometimes u can do a smaller task first if that helps u warm up ur brain juices)
* Pomodoro - do 25 minutes work, 5 minutes rest. Or 40 minutes work, 20 minutes rest. This shifts ur thinking to focus on working time, rather than finishing assignments. Which is a lot less pressure to think about and less daunting. Over time, you may hammer yourself into a studying machine, to the point where u don't need this anymore, but this is a good starting point.
* Library - if u struggle with procrastination and distraction when u go home, try going to the library after school - forces u to be productive b/c less comfortable than ur house. 
* Health - see health section. Good health = focusing is a lot easier and more fun.
* Focus on the present - the present moment is all u have to deal with. All the "work" you have to do is all an abstraction that doesn't exist in reality because reality is only the present moment. If u focus on the present moment only, can make a mountain of homework be more manageable and less daunting, b/c all u have to deal with is the present moment.

### Friendships

In high school you'll meet lots of people, and make some new friends. This is nice. You'll meet people you like, and people you don't like. This is OK. People are different, try not to judge them. If ur a person who struggles with setting boundaries, just cultivate that savage side and just say something whenever u feel like something that someone is doing to u doesn't sit right with u. Most of the time they'll be like "oh my bad". If they don't apologize and continue doing it, that's when u just ditch them. Don't feel bad if u already warned them and they keep doing it. At the end of the day just take care of urself first. It's ok to help other people, as long as ur boundaries don't get infringed upon. But ur boundaries are more important, everyone needs boundaries to live healthily. Maintain ur boundaries, and speak up when they are crossed. Life becomes much nicer to live this way. 

Sometimes there'll be people who are not nice. Sometimes it's just a miscommunication, other times they have some problems they're dealing with (eg. heavily insecure, or dealing with trauma), so don't take it personally. Just try to avoid them.

## Fundraising

To get money, here are the big ways to do it:
1. Competition Donation Matches: The way it works every year is that one (or a few) people book the expenses for the trip, and the club reimburses them. For this reason, every competing member needs to donate their fees to the club so that the club can reimburse the booker. A really smart thing to do is to ask every person to have their parent's company match that donation if their company does that sort of things. This way, let's say 4 people out of 15 competitors can match it, that's literally 4 x $2000 = $8000 of revenue, which is insane. We originally had the idea of everyone paying one person that works at Google, who then donates that to the club. Then, Google can match like $10,000, which is their limit. However, after consulting about this with adults, we concluded that this is a little sus; it's literally fraud, and it might have someone fired from their job or hurt their reputation at the company lol, so may be better to just have individual families match their donations from their respective companies if they can.
2. PTSA/PPIE Grants: This is a good source of income, around $1000 a year. We just have to apply to these grants, and usually we can get this.
3. Karl Schulze: This is one of the OG founders, and he is goated. We want to respect this man for all he has done for this club, and we should always keep in contact. In the past, he has responded to calls for donations, so if you are really tight on money, this is an option. However, treat the man with respect because he has given a ton to the club over the years and not gotten much back, so we have to be better in that respect.
4. Parent Donations: In the beginning of the year, you can send out a letter to have people donate to the club. Hopefully you can raise a few hundred dollars this way.
5. Sponsorships: Sponsorships are tough because companies may not want to give straight up money, but many companies are willing to offer academic discounts on parts or straight up donate parts/services (eg. Theia for their lens, Datron for their CNC machining (cutting metal) services, Cerulean Sonar for their 15% discount on our DVL-75.) According to Calvin, big companies also may have grants lying around that we can apply to.
6. Selling Boba: Ok I put this at the bottom of the list because it's a ton of effort to manage the logistics, and a hard day of work of selling hundreds of bobas at school only amounts to a couple hundred dollars of profit. But, it is still an income source, but keep in mind parts in AVBotz are super expensive (a singular thruster is $1400 ! Now multiply that by 8, and that's only the cost of our thrusters).

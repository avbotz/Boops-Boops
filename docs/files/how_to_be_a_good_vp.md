## How to be a Good VP (Be like craig)

For being a good VP, you need these 3 things:
1. Vision
2. Empathy
3. Hard Work

For vision, you need to have an idea of what you want the subdivision/club to become, and you need to find the practical steps to complete in order to get to that endpoint.

For empathy, you need to be empathetic with your team, to understand them, satisfy their emotional needs, provide the technical support, and build healthy and respectful relationships.

For hard work, don't be afraid to work as hard as anyone on the team, if not more. Don't think of yourself as a manager but instead as a team captain; team captains are still regular working members, but on top of that they are also responsible for leading the rest of the members by giving them tasks to complete and guidance for these tasks. To whom much is given, much is expected.

### Other Tips

Here are some tips learned over the years:
- Start working on day 1, with clear (yet doable, actionable) steps every week. You want to get started asap because competition comes right out of nowhere way faster than you expect, and by then you are pretty clapped if you haven't already been working the whole year.
- Communicate, communicate, communicate! Communication is insanely important to make sure that everyone is on the same page and that there is no communication. Start by thinking of everything possible that you need to do in order to have a working sub, and set deadlines starting in the beginning of the year to get to that setpoint. Communicate with the other divisions to see what they need to do, and communicate with your team so that everyone knows what they should do and how their work helps the sub. 
- Learn as much as you can; never stop learning, as there is always something someone else knows that you don't yet.
- Study other teams, how they construct the sub, how they write their journal paper, how they approach the tasks, etc. for ideas on how to improve. 

### Mental and Physical Health

I can't stress this enough: mental and physical health is the most important thing you can do for yourself to sustain a high level of performance. 

If you want to provide yourself with the energy you need to handle all these responsibilities, you need to invest in yourself and pull back the slingshot so to inject potential energy into yourself. 

There are 4 main habits to stack to improve your energy levels:
1. Exercise
2. Meditation
3. Reading
4. Journaling

Exercise helps with your physical body so that you are able to handle high workloads, meditation helps rest your mind and increases your focus, reading helps expand your mind to think more creatively and effectively, and journaling helps keep your goals in sight and finds ways for you to understand what's going on.

To be able to maintain these habits and not get tired of doing these habits, you think of making them "easy and fun." Make them easy and fun, and they are way easier to maintain. Start with small amounts that are really easy to do daily, such as reading 5 pages, or meditate for 3 minutes or something like that which makes it way easier to maintain every day because it's so easy. 

You also want to get at least 9 hours of sleep every night, to also maintain your energy levels.

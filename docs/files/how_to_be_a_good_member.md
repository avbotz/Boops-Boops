## How to be a Good Member

To be a good member, you just gotta show up to all the meetings, and do the work.

Additionally, if you're done with your work, you can be curious, learn as much as you can from the people around you, and go off on your own to take on responsibilities and tasks that weren't given to you if you think that those are the most beneficial actions that you can do to aid the club/the sub.

The same thing applies as in the VP section, where you want to always take care of your mental and physical health, which are insanely important not just in this club but in your life. You can see that section for tips on how to maintain these two aspects.

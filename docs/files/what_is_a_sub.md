## What is a Sub

From my understanding, a sub is a box with thrusters attached. The thrusters help move the box (or tube) around.

To know where you're at however to know how much to move, you need sensors (DVL, AHRS). To have stuff that is reading the sensors and adjusting thrust values, you need a microcontroller (eg. Arduino). To have something executing a sequence of steps, you need a computer (eg. computer or Jetson). To see competition objects, you need to add cameras that hook up to the computer. Then, to manipulate competition objects, you need to add a grabber, torpedoes, and a dropper.
